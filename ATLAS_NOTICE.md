# Atlas Launcher attribution

Atlas Launcher is a derivative of Prism Launcher and remains licensed under
GPL-3.0-only. Copyright and license notices from Prism Launcher, PolyMC, MultiMC,
and bundled third-party libraries are retained.

Atlas-specific goals are one-click creation of historical Minecraft instances,
Omniarchive-backed archived build discovery, and first-class installation of
community and legacy mod loaders.
